Engineering update

The course desk update says #4471 closed on 5 October 2026. Engineering marked the parser fixed with fix(dates): parse dd/mm for eu locales. It marked the form hint won't fix because booking-v3 was retired. These are different outcomes for two problems that shared one support ticket.

Parser reads month first

The shared parser reads numeric dates month first even when the tenant writes the day first. An ambiguous date can move to another month. In report R-41200, a 7 April reminder became 4 July. This cause spans app, email, and some form reports. The parser issue tracks the fix.

Form hint disagrees with form parsing

For day-first tenants on booking-v3, the form shows MM/DD/YYYY while interpreting the entry by the tenant's day-first convention. The person follows the hint and gets another date. In R-41230, 6 January became 1 June. This cause is specific to the form path. The form hint issue tracks it separately.

How the counts were made

The ticket export has overlapping pages, so one report ID counts once. We include only reports merged into #4471, then use each tenant's locale and booking form with the channel to assign a cause, as implemented in src/lib/kb/causes.ts. Intake tags describe the first guess, not the cause. The five reports in desk-notes.json read as one-day moves are counted under the parser cause by tenant and remain flagged for follow-up; this export does not establish a separate root cause for them.

The accepted subject-line dedupe decision and triage guide can merge different causes. This case shows why the desk should inspect report bodies, tenant locale, form version, and channel before treating a merged ticket as one fix. The old decision record stays as accepted history.
